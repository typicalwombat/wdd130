My Page (Client)

As the client, I decided I wanted to make a website about the history of video games. Included in this page would be a brief timeline of important events in video game history, such as the first console. There would also be another page that served as a guide to collecting video games, including how to properly store and maintain them.


Unfortunately, I was only ever able to met with my lead developer once for this project. Although I handed over my site plan with the basic layout I wanted as well as the content for each page, I never heard back from her and no development occured for my webpage. If there were any problems, I was not made aware of them as she never reached out to me.


As such, my expectations for the site were not met, and I have nothing to show for my personal webpage.


Next time, if issues in communication ever occur with my lead developer, I will take matters into my own hands and directly contact the junior developers and let them know what they need to work on. Nobody ever contacted me about my website, so I believe that they were also never reached out to and instructed on what to do. I will also provide the Junior Developers with a way to contact me directly if they have any problems.

Butterfly Website (Lead Developer)

This project went rather well comparatively. I was able to reach out to my Client and communicate with her about what she wanted for her website. She gave me a basic site layout that I wuickly implemented, and communicated to me the goals of the site and what the two child pages should be about. She decided to do a website about butterflies, so I helped her come up with two pages, one for the butterfly life cycle and one for the different types of butterflies. Once I obtained the wireframe's from her for those two pages, I reached out to my two junior devs, and communicated to them which page they should focus on. I never recieved much information in terms of the content, so with the help of generative AI, I helped compile some information for the Junior Devs to put into their pages.

From the very beginning, I had set up a GitHub repository for my client, and reached out to my junior developers to give them access. I made sure my client had access to the page and could view it as we updated and added information to make sure she could approve it and that she was happy with how everything turned out. There weren't any major complications setting up the webpage other than the images not loading properly while being hosted on GitHub.

My Takeways

The biggest issue with this project was in communicating with the client and the lead developer. More often than not, the client for any given project didn't provide very much information, and it wasn't easy to contact them because they didn't attend class. Often when I wanted to talk to somebody to ask a wuestion, they hadn't shown up to class. In my case as a lead developer, my client frewuently showed up to class, and I had the opprotunity to ask clarifying wuestions. However, in my role as junior developer, across the board it was always "the client hasn't provided us with a site plan yet" or "the client wasn't specific enough in their siteplan." Both of these serving as huge obstacles. In my role as the client, my Lead Developer almost never showed up to class, so there was no opprotunity for follow up. I provided her with a site plan, and asked to her ask me if she had any wuestions, problems or anything else she needed to reach out to me about, and I also provided her with some images, fonts, and other resources I wanted her to use, but nothing ever came of it.

I did enjoy my role as lead developer. I felt the most productive in that role, and I was able to act effectively as a liason between the client and the junior devs to make a website that met the expectations of the client.

If I could do it again, I feel things would have gone differnently if there was better communication, and if everyone showed up to class, allowing us to properly collobarate, cooberate, and communicate effectively with each other, especially if we had any problems or wuestions. I feel that in the real world, this is normally the case, meaning I would know what I'm doing and what's expected of me and be able to recieve feedback about my work.
